One Order, Five Modules

This is the process map behind the 45-minute lab: one customer order threaded live through CRM, Sales, Inventory, Purchase, and Accounting. The diagram below follows Odoo's own screens and button labels — including the one moment the order gets stuck, and which module has to act before it can move again.

Start / End — the order begins or lands
Task — one click a student makes in Odoo
Decision — Odoo checks a condition
Gold arrow — the integration hand-off being taught

The one hand-off that's the point

The gold loop is the whole lesson. Every student's product starts at zero on-hand, so the delivery Odoo creates after Confirm Order is always stuck on "Waiting" — Sales confirmed a promise Inventory can't keep yet. Nothing about Sales is broken; a different module just hasn't acted.

Why the loop resolves itself

Replenish and Confirm PO in Purchase are what unstick it — the moment those ten units are received, the same delivery record flips to "Ready" with no one touching Inventory directly. That's the integration: one action in a different module silently repairs a blocked task without anyone going back to fix it by hand.

Reading this as BPMN

Rounded rectangles are tasks (grouping the clicks each app owns), the diamond is the one decision Odoo makes on its own, and lanes show which app owns each step. The straight arrows are the happy path; the gold arrows are the cross-module dependency the whole 45 minutes is built to surface.
